What changes

The platform makes ambiguity visible.

Technology projects fail in the gray space between what was sold, what was built, what users accepted, and what the business can actually operate. Libertas gives that gray space structure.

  • Every requirement is tied to a contract obligation and acceptance path.
  • Every release request includes evidence, exceptions, and readiness scoring.
  • Every override is visible, intentional, and preserved in the record.
  • Every dispute starts from the same shared facts.

AI-powered, human-led review.

AI accelerates document comparison, missing-evidence detection, test coverage review, defect clustering, and code-risk triage. Human reviewers remain accountable for judgment, context, and final validation.

Banking model

Escrow infrastructure without the investment conflict.

Funds are requested and disbursed through the Libertas Escrow Platform backed by UMB Bank of Kansas City. Libertas is a technology platform and independent validation service built on top of FDIC-insured financial institutions.

What Libertas does

  • Structures escrow workflows for technology projects.
  • Facilitates release requests and evidence review.
  • Provides independent requirements, UAT, code, and go-live validation.
  • Charges a fee on the total escrow account.

What Libertas does not do

  • It does not invest escrow money.
  • It does not collect interest from the escrow account.
  • It does not replace legal counsel or project leadership.
  • It does not force the client to release funds against its judgment.
